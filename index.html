About the school

A place where every child gets a chance to shine.

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Junior High School is located at Lal Sarai, Nagina, District Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh. The school provides education from Nursery through Class 8 in both English and Hindi medium.

From classroom learning to celebrations, creative activities, competitions and community events, our students are encouraged to participate, express themselves and build confidence.

  • Nursery to Class 8 education
  • English and Hindi medium
  • Focus on academics, values and co-curricular development
  • Student participation in celebrations, awareness and cultural activities
Academic journey

Strong foundations for a brighter future.

Our school serves learners from the early years through Class 8, with learning experiences designed to support knowledge, confidence, discipline and creativity.

Early Years

Nursery and foundational learners are encouraged to learn through activity, creativity, interaction and joyful classroom experiences.

Primary Learning

Students build strong academic habits while developing communication, curiosity, discipline and confidence.

Middle School

Classes through VIII encourage deeper understanding, independent thinking, responsibility and preparation for the next stage of education.
